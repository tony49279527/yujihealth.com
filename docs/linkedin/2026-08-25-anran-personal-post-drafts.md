Menstrual Cup OEM Manufacturer China: the RFQ details that make a supplier comparison usable.

When a sourcing team asks only for a unit price, it is difficult to compare two OEM proposals fairly.

For a menstrual-cup project, I recommend putting five items in the first brief:

1. Target market and sales channel.
2. Product direction: size, feel, colour and kit components.
3. Trial quantity or forecast range.
4. Packaging and instructions required for the launch.
5. The documents the buyer needs reviewed before approving a sample.

That does not replace market-specific compliance review. It gives both sides a shared project scope before a quotation, sample plan or document request is discussed.

Sanitary Pad & Liner OEM Manufacturer China: five inputs to include before requesting a private-label quote.

For pads and liners, a category name alone is rarely enough for a sourcing-ready quotation.

The first message is more useful when it identifies:

1. Destination market and channel.
2. Format and intended pack structure.
3. Estimated sample, trial-order or forecast quantity.
4. Packaging, barcode, leaflet and language needs.
5. Which product and market documents need review before purchase.

These inputs help turn an open-ended price request into a project brief that can be checked, clarified and quoted. Product requirements and market claims still need to be reviewed for the specific project.
