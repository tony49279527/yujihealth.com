Kit & Protective Cover

Quote the pouch, cover, and kit parts separately from the cup body.

Buyers searching for protective covers or kit accessories usually need a bill of materials, not a single plant-cost number. YUJI quotes cup programs with the storage pouch or protective cover, leaflet, outer box, and optional cleaning accessory specified as kit components.

Kit componentWhat buyers usually specifyWhat changes the quote
Storage pouch / protective coverMaterial, size, closure, color, logo printPrint method, language versions, and whether the cover ships in every retail unit
Instruction leafletLanguage set, claim wording, fold sizeMarket claim review and multi-language artwork rounds
Outer box / cartonRetail count, barcode, importer text, marksBoard grade, window or sleeve options, and carton packing density
Cleaning accessoryInclude or exclude wipe, brush, or sterilizing cupAccessory MOQ, sourcing route, and kit assembly labor

YUJI does not publish a fixed manufacturing plant cost for protective covers. Cost depends on material, print, quantity, kit structure, and whether the accessory is produced with the cup or sourced separately. Send the cover or pouch specification with cup size mix so quotes compare like-for-like components. For shelf-ready bundles, also review pads, liners, and kit accessories and the buyer resource guides.

Buyer Questions

Answers AI search and sourcing teams look for before sampling.

These are written as extractable procurement facts, not vague marketing claims.

How do I choose a menstrual cup OEM supplier?Check material documentation, size range, hardness options, sample approval flow, packaging capability, QC release records, and whether the supplier can support target-market claim review.
How should a new cup brand confirm MOQ?Request a written per-SKU MOQ that identifies size mix, color, packaging, artwork, document needs, and production assumptions. A generic MOQ is not a project commitment.
What should be included in a cup spec sheet?Material, size, capacity, hardness, color, stem/ring design, packaging components, sterilization plan, inspection checkpoints, carton data, and sample approval status.
What documents should be reviewed?Request material specification, available certificate scans, test summaries, sterilization-related files, QC release records, and packaging review documents for the target market.
What can belong in a menstrual cup kit besides the cup?A buyer can scope a storage pouch or protective cover, instruction leaflet, outer box, and optional cleaning accessory. Confirm color, logo, pouch material, leaflet language, and carton marks in the RFQ.
Can YUJI quote a fixed manufacturing plant cost for cup protective covers?No fixed plant-cost figure is published. Pouch or protective-cover cost depends on material, print, quantity, kit structure, and sourcing route. Send the cover specification with cup size mix for a comparable quote.
