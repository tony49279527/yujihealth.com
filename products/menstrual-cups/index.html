Customization

What can be discussed for a private-label program.

Customization for menstrual cups is handled as a project discussion, not as an open-ended menu. Each request is reviewed against the quoted specification, material direction, tooling, packaging, document scope, and production plan.

  • Color: Color direction is available for project discussion against quoted material and production plan.
  • Size direction: Reference S/M/L and project-specific reference size planning are available for project discussion.
  • Logo: Private-label logo placement is available for project discussion; artwork approval is required.
  • Pouch: Pouch direction and printed pouch artwork are available for project discussion.
  • Box: Retail box or kit box direction is available for project discussion.
  • Leaflet language: Leaflet language and content direction are available for project discussion; claim boundaries must be agreed.
  • Kit components: Kit components are available for project discussion and must be tied to the quoted BOM.

Customization is not open-ended. Two points are fixed: claim boundaries must be agreed before artwork approval, and a written production plan is issued only after product, artwork, packaging, document, inspection, and shipping terms are agreed. Product scope and document availability are confirmed project by project.

Sample & Approval

From brief to approved production.

The YUJI OEM/ODM project path is brief and sample, customize and approve, trial production, and mass delivery.

  1. Brief and sample: Confirm market, channel, product, volume, packaging, and document needs.
  2. Customize and approve: Review product direction, color, packaging, artwork, and sample approval.
  3. Trial production: Verify dimensions, hardness or format, pack fit, process controls, and buyer records.
  4. Mass delivery: Manage production, QC sampling, release files, cartons, and export handoff.

For menstrual cups, the sample review process starts with buyer decisions: intended user, size direction, color, logo, pouch, box, leaflet language, and kit components. YUJI then reviews the quoted specification, sample feel, QC checkpoints, and claim boundaries before artwork approval. Buyers should ask which current material, test, certificate-scope, and release records may be available for the quoted project.

Key confirmation points before mass production include the approved product specification, sample feel, QC checkpoints, claim boundaries, artwork and packaging approval, document scope, inspection plan, shipping terms, and written production plan.

For planning only, industry references commonly seen are: catalog samples 7–14 days; paid sample sets 3–10 pieces, 7–15 business days; prototype-mold samples 15–25 days; catalog cup bulk production 25–40 calendar days after packaging approval and deposit. These are industry-typical references, not YUJI commitments. YUJI does not publish lead times in the line sheet; timing is confirmed per project.

For planning only, industry catalog cup OEM MOQ often starts around 500 units per SKU; custom printed pouches or cartons often 500+ units per design; new custom molds are usually discussed at 500+ units to amortize tooling.
