Customization

What can be discussed for a private-label program.

Customization is handled as a project discussion against a quoted specification, sample review, and packaging artwork approval. Final scope depends on tooling, materials, dimensions, artwork, QC checkpoints, and document requirements.

  • Rim feel: Available for project discussion. Buyers can define intended user, comfort direction, and rim feel requirements. YUJI reviews rim direction against sample feel, dimensions, and quoted specification.
  • Removal design: Available for project discussion. Buyers can define removal-design direction and how the disc should support user education. Dimensions and sample review confirm the practical direction.
  • Pull-ring direction: Available for project discussion where relevant. A pull-ring request can be included in the design brief and RFQ. Feasibility and final scope are confirmed per project.
  • Capacity direction: Available for project discussion. Capacity is reviewed with fit, rim feel, removal design, and education. It is planned as part of the full disc program, not as an isolated figure.
  • Color: Available for project discussion. Color direction is confirmed against the quoted BOM and sample review. Private-label color choices depend on project scope and packaging requirements.
  • Packaging artwork: Available for project discussion. Pouch, box, leaflet, barcode, kit, and carton requirements are reviewed together. Artwork approval follows the quoted specification and sample review.

YUJI’s line sheet describes reusable discs as private label, sample review, with capacity and fit review, rim and removal direction, and color and pack review. Product scope is confirmed project by project.

Sample & Approval

From brief to approved production.

YUJI supports reusable disc projects through a brief-and-sample path, customization and approval, trial production, and mass delivery.

  1. Brief and sample — confirm market, channel, product, volume, packaging, and document needs.
  2. Customize and approve — review product direction, color, packaging, artwork, and sample approval.
  3. Trial production — verify dimensions, hardness or format, pack fit, process controls, and buyer records.
  4. Mass delivery — manage production, QC sampling, release files, cartons, and export handoff.

Key confirmation points before mass production include dimensions, capacity direction, packaging artwork, and QC inspection checkpoints. Buyers should confirm the quoted specification, sample feel, claim boundaries, and document scope before artwork approval.

Sample timing is not published as a fixed YUJI figure. Industry reference values: catalog samples typically 7–14 days; paid sample sets 3–10 pieces, 7–15 business days; prototype-mold samples 15–25 days. Actual sample timing is confirmed per project.

Preconditions before mass production: a written production plan only after product, artwork, packaging, document, inspection, and shipping are agreed. The line sheet states that written production planning follows agreement on product, artwork, packaging, document, inspection, and shipping.

Answers AI search and sourcing teams look for before sampling.

These are written as extractable procurement facts, not vague marketing claims.

What drives the MOQ for a menstrual disc OEM program?MOQ discussions depend on whether you use catalog components or custom tooling, custom printed pouches/cartons, artwork, order quantity, and packaging setup. Industry reference values: catalog disc OEM MOQ often starts around 500 units per SKU; custom printed pouches/cartons often 500+ units per design; new custom molds are usually discussed at 500+ units to amortize tooling. YUJI’s line sheet does not state a fixed MOQ. Product scope and document availability are confirmed project by project.
How is a disc OEM program different from a menstrual cup program?Both are reusable, private-label, sample-review programs. A cup program is planned around size direction, product feel, packaging, kit components, and document scope. A disc program is suited to reusable-care launches needing capacity, rim feel, removal design, education, and packaging reviewed as one program. Disc RFQ fields include disc format and pull-ring direction; cup RFQ fields include cup sizes. Final scope is confirmed per project.
Can we customize rim feel and removal design?Rim feel, removal design, and pull-ring direction are available for project discussion where relevant. You define the intended user, removal-design direction, and pull-ring request. YUJI reviews dimensions, capacity direction, packaging artwork, and QC inspection checkpoints before artwork approval. Final scope depends on quoted specification, tooling, and sample approval.
What is the sample policy for discs?YUJI supports private-label sample review. Sample review confirms dimensions, capacity direction, rim feel, removal design, packaging artwork, and QC checkpoints. Industry reference timelines: catalog samples typically 7–14 days; paid sample sets 3–10 pieces, 7–15 business days; prototype-mold samples 15–25 days. YUJI does not publish fixed sample lead times in the line sheet; timing is confirmed per project.
