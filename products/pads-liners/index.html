Specification Gate

Define the pad material stack before comparing quotes.

A material name alone is not a finished specification. Ask suppliers to connect every proposed layer, performance target, and packaging claim to the quoted SKU and an agreed verification file.

Decision areaBuyer inputVerification requestClaim boundary

Absorbent coreCore direction, target absorption, rewet expectations, pad lengthAgreed sample, product specification, test method, and dated result where availableDo not use a universal absorption number across formats or test methods

Sourcing Questions

Disposable and accessory programs depend on pack economics.

Buyers compare these SKUs by assortment value, MOQ, wrapper design, carton efficiency, and whether they strengthen the core reusable-product launch.

What affects sanitary pad OEM pricing?Length, absorbency target, top sheet, wrapper, retail count, outer box, carton data, test file needs, and order quantity all affect quotation quality.
How do accessories support menstrual cup or disc kits?Pouches, wipes, sterilizing cups, and cleaning kits can help buyers assemble a more complete starter pack when the accessory bill of materials, instructions, and claim scope are reviewed together.
What should buyers define first?Target market, price tier, retail count, product dimensions, packaging language, accessory mix, and document requirements should be fixed before quote comparison.
Which document files are useful?Ask for product format specifications, the packaging bill of materials, scope-checked available test or certificate files, carton data, and QC release records.
