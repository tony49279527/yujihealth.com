Product lines

What each program is planned around.

Menstrual cups

Cup programs are planned around buyer size direction, product feel, packaging, kit components, and the document scope needed for the intended market. Reference sizes S / M / L; capacity and feel reviewed per project; pouch and pack direction for private label.

  • Define the intended user, size direction, color, logo, pouch, box, leaflet language, and kit components.
  • Review the quoted specification, sample feel, QC checkpoints, and claim boundaries before artwork approval.
  • Ask which current material, test, certificate-scope, and release records may be available for the quoted project.
  • Request a written production plan only after product, artwork, packaging, document, inspection, and shipping requirements are agreed.

Reusable menstrual discs

Disc projects are suited to reusable-care launches that need capacity, rim feel, removal design, education, and packaging reviewed as one product program. Send in the RFQ: target market, channel, disc format, expected quantity, color, pull-ring direction, packaging, samples, document list, and timeline.

  • Define rim feel and removal-design direction, including a pull-ring request where relevant.
  • Plan education and packaging for DTC, Amazon, pharmacy, or premium wellness channels.
  • Confirm dimensions, capacity direction, packaging artwork requirements, and QC inspection checkpoints.
  • Request current material, certificate, test, and release records for the quoted product and market.

Pads, liners and accessories

Disposable and auxiliary SKUs support broader retail shelves, reusable-care bundles, travel kits, distributor catalogs, and regional trial programs. Send in the RFQ: target market, format, material direction, retail count, order quantity, wrapper and box needs, carton requirements, document list, and timeline.

  • Define pad or liner format, absorbency direction, topsheet, wrapper, count, and retail pack.
  • Confirm material positioning and market-facing claims only against the quoted BOM and supporting documentation.
  • Use accessories such as storage pouches, wipes, sterilizing cups, and cleaning kits to build assortments.
  • Request product specification, packaging bill of materials, carton data, test files, and QC release records.

OEM / ODM project path

From brief to export handoff.

  1. Brief and sample. Confirm market, channel, product, volume, packaging, and document needs.
  2. Customize and approve. Review product direction, color, packaging, artwork, and sample approval.
  3. Trial production. Verify dimensions, hardness or format, pack fit, process controls, and buyer records.
  4. Mass delivery. Manage production, QC sampling, release files, cartons, and export handoff.

Evidence buyers can request

Material specifications, product dimensions or format, available certificate scans, test summaries, first-article and in-process records, QC release, packaging files, carton data, and export handoff records.

FAQ

Questions procurement teams ask about the line sheet.

What does the YUJI line sheet cover?

The line sheet summarizes the company profile, three product lines (menstrual cups, reusable discs, pads/liners and accessories), the OEM/ODM project path, the evidence buyers can request, and the RFQ fields sourcing teams should send. A PDF version is available for download.
