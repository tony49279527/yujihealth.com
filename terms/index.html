Terms Overview

This page covers website use, RFQ submission terms, inquiry handling, confidentiality, and limitations of liability for buyers engaging with YUJI through yujihealth.com. Submitting an RFQ is a non-binding inquiry.

RFQ and inquiry submissions

Submitting an RFQ form or sending an email to YUJI constitutes a non-binding business inquiry. YUJI reviews each inquiry and responds with relevant product specifications, MOQ references, pricing guidance, and next steps where applicable.
