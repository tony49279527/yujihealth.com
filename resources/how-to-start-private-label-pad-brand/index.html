Brand Launch Guide

How to start a private label sanitary pad brand: a procurement step-by-step guide.

Launching a private-label sanitary pad brand requires planning across product format, material selection, MOQ strategy, packaging design, OEM partner evaluation, and logistics coordination. This guide walks procurement teams through each stage with actionable specifications and supplier discussion points.

Last reviewed: August 10, 2026 · Scope: conventional pads and liners
Brand Launch Guide

A successful private-label pad launch starts with a clear retail product definition, honest MOQ planning, and a partner who shares transparent material and QC documentation. YUJI recommends defining your core SKU range, packaging specification, and timeline before issuing the first RFQ. The steps below cover everything from format selection to first shipment.

Procurement Process

Step 1: Define your product format and absorbency range.

Start by deciding which pad lengths and absorbency levels your brand will offer. Common launch categories include panty liners (140-160 mm, light absorbency), day pads (190-240 mm, moderate absorbency), and overnight pads (280-420 mm, high absorbency). Maternity pads and post-surgery pads serve specific market segments. Most brands launch with 2-3 core lengths to test channel response, then expand based on sell-through data.

Procurement discussion point: Ask your OEM partner which formats they produce most frequently in your target market. Volume-optimized formats often have better pricing and shorter lead times.

Step 2: Select top sheet, core, and back sheet materials.

Material selection determines retail positioning and cost structure. Top sheet options include cotton (comfort positioning), organic cotton (premium/sustainable), bamboo charcoal (odor-control claims), and synthetic non-woven (value tier). Absorbent core options include superabsorbent polymer (SAP), wood fluff pulp, or blended constructions. Back sheet choices include breathable PE film or non-woven fabric.

Procurement discussion point: Request material specification sheets for each component and confirm traceability from the OEM's suppliers. Ask about minimum material order quantities and lead time differences between material tiers.

Step 3: Plan your MOQ and phasing strategy.

Confirm a written per-SKU MOQ after format, retail pack, wrapper, material, and customization are defined. Plan first-order quantities per SKU and retail pack count. A phased launch can reduce initial inventory risk and allow demand validation.

Procurement discussion point: Ask whether the OEM offers combined MOQ across multiple SKUs or reduced MOQ for repeat orders of the same format.

Step 4: Design packaging — wrapper, box, carton, and barcode.

Retail packaging includes individual wrapper design (sealed, folded, or perforated), retail box or pouch configuration, carton pack quantity, and barcode assignment. Specify packaging language, any required warning or importer text, and sustainability preferences (recyclable or biodegradable materials). Packaging artwork typically requires 1-2 revision rounds — factor this into your launch timeline.

Procurement discussion point: Confirm the OEM's artwork specification format (AI, PDF, or layered files), the number of revision rounds included in the quoted price, and the lead time impact of custom packaging versus standard options.

Step 5: Request samples and evaluate against specifications.

Request pre-production samples for each format and verify pad length, absorbency performance, material layering alignment, wrapper seal quality, box or pouch fit, and overall print quality. Compare every sample against your written specification sheet. Document all approvals in writing before authorizing mass production.

Procurement discussion point: Ask about sample lead time, whether sample cost is deductible from the first production order, and how the OEM handles specification revisions during the sampling phase.

Step 6: Confirm quality documentation and certificate readiness.

Request component specifications for the top sheet, core, back sheet, adhesive, and wrapper, then identify the exact finished-product or material tests required for the intended use and destination market. For every certificate or report, verify the holder, issuing body or laboratory, scope, issue and expiry dates, tested sample, method, result, and whether the file is approved for your use.

Regulatory boundary: In the United States, determine the applicable product code, class, establishment or device-listing duties, and any exemption limitations; registration or listing does not mean FDA approval. In the European Union, use CE marking only when legislation covering the product specifically requires it. Review the FDA registration guidance and EU CE guidance, then confirm the route with the importer and qualified regulatory counsel.

Step 7: Plan logistics — carton, shipping, and market entry.

Confirm carton dimensions and pack quantity, carton labeling and marking requirements, and Incoterm agreement with your OEM. Coordinate with your freight forwarder on shipping timelines, required customs documentation, and destination market import requirements such as labeling language, ingredient declarations, and product registration procedures.

Buyer FAQ

Common questions about starting a private-label sanitary pad brand.

What is the minimum order quantity for starting a private-label sanitary pad brand?

Confirm a written per-SKU MOQ after format complexity, retail pack design, wrapper type, material choices, and customization are defined. A phased launch can reduce initial inventory risk.

How much does it cost to start a private-label pad brand from China?

Cost depends on format count, material tier, packaging complexity, and MOQ. Budget for product cost per unit, tooling or mold fees if applicable, packaging development and artwork, sample production and shipping, and certificate or testing costs. Request a detailed quotation from the OEM that itemizes each component.

How long does it take to launch a private-label sanitary pad brand?

Build the schedule from quotation, sample approval, artwork approval, testing or registration needs, production slot, inspection, and shipping milestones confirmed by the selected supplier and logistics partners.

What pad formats should I launch first?

A buyer can test a focused range such as a day pad and an overnight pad before adding liners or specialty formats. Choose lengths and absorbency targets from channel evidence, user requirements, and the final specification rather than treating one launch mix as universal.

What certifications do I need to import private-label pads from China?

Requirements depend on intended use, claims, product classification, destination market, and each operator's role. In the US, determine the applicable product code, class, registration or listing duties, and any exemption limitations; registration does not mean FDA approval. In the EU, CE marking is used only when legislation covering the product specifically requires it. Confirm the route with the importer and qualified regulatory counsel.
