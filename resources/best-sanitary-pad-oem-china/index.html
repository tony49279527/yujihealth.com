Procurement Overview

China's sanitary pad OEM landscape for procurement teams.

China's sanitary pad manufacturing sector includes large-scale producers with fully automated high-speed lines and mid-size manufacturers offering more format flexibility. Buyers evaluating Chinese pad OEM manufacturers should compare four dimensions: format range and absorbency options, top sheet and core material sourcing, retail packaging capability, and quality system and export certification coverage.

Format range. Liners (140–160 mm), day pads (190–240 mm), overnight (280–420 mm), maternity and specialty. Absorbency and core layering vary by target use. A liner buyer may need thinness and comfort; an overnight buyer may need length, leakage protection, and SAP distribution; a maternity buyer may need larger coverage and softer surface.

Material choice. Cotton, organic cotton , bamboo charcoal, or synthetic top sheets. SAP, wood fluff, or blended cores. Breathable PE or non-woven back sheets. Each material direction changes cost, lead time , certificate availability, and claim language.

Pack capabilities. Individual wrapper, multi-pack box, barcode, carton configuration, and multilingual packaging design all affect lead time and cost. Ask whether the supplier handles artwork adaptation, dieline creation, barcode verification, and carton drop-test planning.

Market and claim route. Determine classification and operator duties first, then verify only the certificates , tests, or registrations that apply to the quoted product and claim. Official starting points include FDA registration and listing guidance and EU CE-marking guidance . Confirm the current product-specific route with the importer and responsible regulatory parties. The supplier can support documents, but the buyer remains responsible for market access decisions.

Supplier archetype 2: High-volume automated pad producer.

This archetype may suit a buyer with stable forecasts and standardized formats. Do not infer capacity, unit cost, run minimum, or QC depth from factory size or location; verify the actual line, quoted SKU, material changeover, MOQ , production slot, and records.

Best fit for: Established retail brands, supermarket chains, and institutional buyers who can commit to long production runs with limited format or material variation between orders.

Procurement caution: Confirm minimum run length per format , assess whether the factory can switch between material types (e.g., cotton to bamboo charcoal) without minimum charge , and verify QC documentation standards match your regulatory requirements. High-volume producers may offer attractive unit pricing at scale, but flexibility can be limited. If your brand needs frequent pack-count changes, seasonal artwork, or multiple material tiers, ask how changeovers affect schedule, waste, and cost.

Supplier archetype 3: Certified-material or specialty-format supplier.

This archetype may suit a project using a certified material claim or specialized format. Verify that the exact material, certificate holder , transaction or chain-of-custody documents, finished product, labeling language, and destination-market rules support the proposed claim. A certificate for raw material does not automatically support a finished-product claim, and a scheme certificate may have scope limits.

Best fit for: Brands launching organic or eco-positioned product lines, buyers targeting Europe or North America with specific sustainability claims, and distributors needing certified supply chains .

Procurement caution: Verify certification scope matches your target market, confirm organic material sourcing capacity for your volume range, and review whether the manufacturer can integrate specialty pads with a broader feminine-care assortment. Specialty suppliers can be strong partners for claim-led products, but the evidence must line up from raw material to retail label.

How to choose the right pad OEM manufacturer.

1. Define the retail product. Specify pad length, absorbency, top sheet and core materials, wrapper design, retail pack count, and carton configuration before approaching manufacturers. Include target price band, target market, claims, and launch volume. A clear one-page spec sheet reduces back-and-forth and makes quotes comparable.

2. Compare material transparency. Request top sheet, core, back sheet, and wrapper material specifications and available certificates for each quoted product. Ask for lot or batch traceability examples. If a claim depends on a material, confirm the chain of custody from raw material to finished pad.

3. Assess packaging capability. Manufacturers with in-house artwork and packaging design support reduce development cycles. Request packaging examples during screening. Confirm who owns dielines, who checks barcodes, who approves language, and how carton strength is validated.

4. Review QC documentation. Compare how each manufacturer documents raw material inspection, in-process checks, final inspection, and batch release records. Ask for a sample QC plan for a similar SKU, not just a generic quality policy.

5. Confirm the legal and claim route. Verify product classification, operator duties, certificate or test scope, dates, and permitted wording instead of using a universal FDA , CE , ISO , or organic checklist. The importer, legal counsel, and regulatory consultant should confirm the final route. The supplier supports documents; the buyer owns the market decision.

6. Pressure-test the volume plan. Ask for per-SKU MOQ , trial run options, repeat-order minimums, and changeover rules. Confirm whether packaging components, material switches, or custom colors reset the MOQ . Align the MOQ with realistic sell-through, not just launch ambition.

7. Lock the schedule in writing. Ask for a dated production slot tied to approved materials, format, packaging artwork, testing or registration needs, inspection, and shipping. Do not rely on a general lead-time promise. Build buffer for sample approval, Chinese public holidays, and shipping peaks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How should buyers confirm MOQ for a sanitary pad OEM program?

Ask each supplier for a written per-SKU MOQ that identifies format, retail pack, wrapper, material, and customization assumptions. Do not use a generic MOQ as a quotation or launch-plan commitment. Confirm whether the MOQ applies to pads only, or also to printed boxes, pouches, cartons, and labels.

What pad formats can Chinese OEM manufacturers produce?

Chinese OEM manufacturers typically produce panty liners, day pads, overnight pads, maternity pads, and post-surgery pads. Formats vary by length (140 mm to 420 mm), absorbency rating, wing configuration, top sheet material (cotton, bamboo charcoal, or synthetic), and wrapper style. Ask for a format matrix with exact dimensions and absorbency values.

Which certifications should a sanitary pad OEM manufacturer in China have?

Required evidence depends on intended use, claims, product classification, destination market, and operator roles. For the US, determine the applicable product code, class, registration or listing duties, and any exemption limitations; registration is not FDA approval . For the EU, use CE marking only when applicable legislation specifically requires it. Verify every material or scheme certificate against the quoted product and permitted claim. YUJI's document availability must be confirmed per project.

How do I compare quotes from different sanitary pad OEM suppliers?

Compare quotes by reviewing pad length and absorbency specifications, top sheet and core material clarity, retail pack count and wrapper design, artwork support capability, carton configuration, QC inspection criteria, and which material safety certificates accompany the quote. Without these details, unit price comparison is misleading. Ask every supplier to quote the same BOM, pack count, and Incoterm.

What is the typical lead time for a sanitary pad OEM order from China?

Do not use an industry-wide lead-time assumption. Ask each supplier for a dated production-slot estimate tied to approved materials, format, packaging artwork, testing or registration needs, inspection, and shipping. Industry-typical pad bulk production is often quoted at 35–40 days , but your project may differ. Confirm the schedule in writing.

What material documentation should I request for top sheet, core, and back sheet?

Request material specifications, supplier declarations, safety data or test summaries where applicable, and lot traceability examples. For absorbency, ask about SAP type, fluff pulp, core layering, and finished-product performance testing. For skin-contact materials, ask about biocompatibility or irritation testing if your market route requires it . Match every document to the quoted BOM and SKU.

What packaging details should be confirmed before sending a pad RFQ?

Confirm individual wrapper type and size, multi-pack box dimensions, pack count, barcode format and placement, carton configuration, language requirements, and artwork ownership. Ask whether the supplier provides dieline creation, artwork adaptation, barcode verification, and packaging samples. Packaging readiness often determines whether a launch stays on schedule .

How should buyers verify organic or specialty pad claims?

Verify that the exact material, certificate holder , transaction or chain-of-custody documents, finished product, labeling language, and destination-market rules support the proposed claim. A raw-material certificate does not automatically support a finished-product claim. Confirm the scope, date, and permitted wording with the certificate owner and your regulatory advisor.
