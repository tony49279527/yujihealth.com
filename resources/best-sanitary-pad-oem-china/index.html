Supplier archetype 2: High-volume automated pad producer.

This archetype may suit a buyer with stable forecasts and standardized formats. Do not infer capacity, unit cost, run minimum, or QC depth from factory size or location; verify the actual line, quoted SKU, material changeover, MOQ, production slot, and records.

Best fit for: Established retail brands, supermarket chains, and institutional buyers who can commit to long production runs with limited format or material variation between orders.

Procurement caution: Confirm minimum run length per format, assess whether the factory can switch between material types (e.g., cotton to bamboo charcoal) without minimum charge, and verify QC documentation standards match your regulatory requirements.

Supplier archetype 3: Certified-material or specialty-format supplier.

This archetype may suit a project using a certified material claim or specialized format. Verify that the exact material, certificate holder, transaction or chain-of-custody documents, finished product, labeling language, and destination-market rules support the proposed claim.

Best fit for: Brands launching organic or eco-positioned product lines, buyers targeting Europe or North America with specific sustainability claims, and distributors needing certified supply chains.

Procurement caution: Verify certification scope matches your target market, confirm organic material sourcing capacity for your volume range, and review whether the manufacturer can integrate specialty pads with a broader feminine-care assortment.

How to choose the right pad OEM manufacturer.

  1. Define the retail product. Specify pad length, absorbency, top sheet and core materials, wrapper design, retail pack count, and carton configuration before approaching manufacturers.
  2. Compare material transparency. Request top sheet, core, back sheet, and wrapper material specifications and available certificates for each quoted product.
  3. Assess packaging capability. Manufacturers with in-house artwork and packaging design support reduce development cycles. Request packaging examples during screening.
  4. Review QC documentation. Compare how each manufacturer documents raw material inspection, in-process checks, final inspection, and batch release records.
  5. Confirm the legal and claim route. Verify product classification, operator duties, certificate or test scope, dates, and permitted wording instead of using a universal FDA, CE, ISO, or organic checklist.

Buyer FAQ

Common questions about sanitary pad OEM manufacturers in China.

What is the typical MOQ for private-label sanitary pads from Chinese OEM manufacturers?

YUJI uses 10,000 pieces per SKU as a trial MOQ reference for sanitary pads and liners. MOQ may vary based on format complexity, retail pack design, wrapper type, and material choices such as organic cotton top sheet or specialty absorbent cores.

What pad formats can Chinese OEM manufacturers produce?

Chinese OEM manufacturers typically produce panty liners, day pads, overnight pads, maternity pads, and post-surgery pads. Formats vary by length (140 mm to 420 mm), absorbency rating, wing configuration, top sheet material (cotton, bamboo charcoal, or synthetic), and wrapper style.

Which certifications should a sanitary pad OEM manufacturer in China have?

Required evidence depends on intended use, claims, product classification, destination market, and operator roles. For the US, determine the applicable product code, class, registration or listing duties, and any exemption limitations; registration is not FDA approval. For the EU, use CE marking only when applicable legislation specifically requires it. Verify every material or scheme certificate against the quoted product and permitted claim.

How do I compare quotes from different sanitary pad OEM suppliers?

Compare quotes by reviewing pad length and absorbency specifications, top sheet and core material clarity, retail pack count and wrapper design, artwork support capability, carton configuration, QC inspection criteria, and which material safety certificates accompany the quote. Without these details, unit price comparison is misleading.

What is the typical lead time for a sanitary pad OEM order from China?

Do not use an industry-wide lead-time assumption. Ask each supplier for a dated production-slot estimate tied to approved materials, format, packaging artwork, testing or registration needs, inspection, and shipping.
