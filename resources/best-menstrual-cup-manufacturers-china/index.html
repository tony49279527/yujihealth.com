How to choose the right manufacturer for your procurement stage.

  1. Define your volume tier. First-year forecasts under 10,000 units suit flexible MOQ suppliers. Multi-year volume commitments can unlock pricing at higher-output factories.
  2. Assess document needs. Regulated markets (FDA, CE) require deeper material and biocompatibility files. Confirm document availability before the RFQ.
  3. Check packaging readiness. Factories with in-house packaging design reduce development cycles. Request packaging capability examples during supplier screening.
  4. Request QC records. Compare how each manufacturer documents dimensional checks, visual inspection, sterilization records, and batch release criteria.
  5. Verify communication flow. English-speaking project management and responsive RFQ handling reduce sampling and production delays.

Buyer FAQ

Common questions about menstrual cup manufacturers in China.

What is the typical MOQ for menstrual cup OEM from a Chinese manufacturer?

YUJI uses 1,000 pieces as a trial MOQ reference for menstrual cup programs. MOQ varies by manufacturer based on color count, size range, packaging complexity, and customization depth. Always confirm MOQ per SKU before quoting artwork and tooling.

Which certifications should a menstrual cup manufacturer in China hold?

Request ISO 13485 (medical device quality management), FDA establishment registration or 510(k) evidence, CE marking under MDR or MDD, and biocompatibility test records (ISO 10993 or USP Class VI). Material certificates for medical-grade liquid silicone rubber (LSR) should also be available.

Can a single Chinese manufacturer produce both menstrual cups and discs?

Some manufacturers, including YUJI, can produce both cups and discs under the same quality system. Buyers planning a dual-SKU reusable launch should confirm the manufacturer's mold capability, silicone processing lines, and packaging support for both product types before quoting.

How do I compare quotes from different menstrual cup manufacturers in China?

Compare quotes by reviewing material specification clarity, S/M/L sizing dimensions and capacities, hardness options, color and logo capability, packaging components (pouch, box, leaflet), QC release points, and which certifications accompany each quote. Unit price alone is not a reliable comparison metric.

What is the typical lead time for a menstrual cup OEM order from China?

Regular orders are often planned around 15-20 working days after sample and artwork approvals. Lead time depends on color selection, packaging design, sterilization requirements, and whether the mold already exists or requires development.

Should I visit a menstrual cup factory in China before placing an order?

Factory visits are recommended for first-time buyers placing volume orders. If a physical visit is not possible, request a video walkthrough of the injection molding, quality inspection, and cleanroom packaging areas, and ask for live QC records from a recent production batch.
