FAQ

Common buyer questions before sampling.

What material and testing records can buyers review for menstrual cups?

Request the quoted material specification and identify any material or finished-product test report required for the intended use. Verify the tested sample, method, result, date, scope, and approval status before using a material or safety claim.

How should buyers choose cup sizes?

Size labels vary by supplier. Compare measured dimensions, verified capacity data, product structure, intended-user research, and the approved specification revision before choosing names or publishing fit guidance.

Can YUJI provide samples?

Confirm sample availability, quantity, specification revision, evaluation plan, cost, freight responsibility, and approval criteria in the quotation. A sample is an acceptance baseline, not proof of later batch conformity.

How should I confirm production timing?

Confirm a dated production plan only after material, sample, artwork, document, packaging, inspection, and shipping requirements are agreed. A generic lead-time range is not a project commitment.

Can packaging and formulas be customized?

Ask which existing structures, colors, logo methods, packaging components, or formulations can be quoted for the specific product. Custom work, minimums, tests, ownership, and lead time must be agreed by project.

Which documents can buyers request before ordering?

Ask YUJI to identify the exact specifications, certificate scans, test summaries, QC release records, sterilization-related files, packaging documents, and carton data available for the quoted project, including scope and approval status.
