Procurement Overview

China's private-label feminine care supplier landscape.

China's feminine care private-label market includes specialized silicone manufacturers (menstrual cups and discs), absorbent hygiene product factories (sanitary pads and liners), and full-range suppliers that produce both categories. Buyers launching a branded feminine care line should evaluate suppliers across product scope, material control, certification coverage, packaging integration, and quality system maturity.

Supplier type 2: Category-specialized manufacturers.

Many Chinese manufacturers focus exclusively on either silicone reusables (cups and discs) or absorbent hygiene products (pads and liners). These suppliers often offer deeper expertise in their category but require buyers to manage multiple supplier relationships for a full product range.

Best fit for: Buyers launching a single-category product line who want category-specific depth, specialized material expertise, and potentially lower pricing within that product type.

Procurement caution: Assess whether managing separate suppliers for each category aligns with your quality system, document management, and logistics resources. Multi-supplier programs require more coordination during sampling, production, and shipping.

Supplier type 3: Large hygiene product groups with diversified output.

Large Chinese hygiene product groups produce pads, liners, diapers, and adult incontinence products across high-speed manufacturing lines. These groups offer scale pricing and broad certification coverage but may have less flexibility for small-batch private-label feminine care programs.

Best fit for: Retail chains and institutional buyers with high-volume forecasts and standardized product specifications who prioritize unit cost over format flexibility.

Procurement caution: Verify the manufacturer's minimum engagement level for private-label programs versus branded production, confirm format and material change flexibility, and review whether feminine care receives dedicated production line allocation.

How to select the right private-label supplier for your program.

  1. Map your product roadmap. Define which SKUs you need now and which you plan to add in the next 12-24 months. A multi-category supplier protects against switching costs later.
  2. Compare quality system scope. Confirm the supplier's ISO registration covers the product types in your program. Request certificate scope documents, not just certificate numbers.
  3. Evaluate packaging integration. Suppliers offering in-house packaging design reduce artwork revision cycles. Request examples of retail-ready packaging from their current private-label clients.
  4. Review document management. Assess whether the supplier can provide consistent specification sheets, QC records, and certificate packages across all your product types.
  5. Test communication responsiveness. Sample RFQ response time, question clarity, and documentation quality. These indicators predict the production-phase experience.

Buyer FAQ

Common questions about feminine care private label in China.

What feminine care products can suppliers in China private-label?

Chinese suppliers can private-label menstrual cups (silicone, S/M/L sizing), menstrual discs, sanitary pads and liners (various lengths, absorbencies, and materials), intimate wipes, cleansing foams, reusable pouch and starter kits, and accessory items such as cup steamers and storage cases.

What MOQ should I expect for a private-label feminine care program from China?

YUJI uses 1,000 pcs as a trial MOQ reference for menstrual cups and 10,000 pcs for sanitary pads. MOQ varies by product type, customization depth, packaging complexity, and whether the supplier has existing molds or requires new tooling.

Can one supplier handle my full feminine care product range?

Suppliers with diversified production capabilities — such as YUJI, which produces cups, discs, pads, and accessories — can manage multi-SKU programs under a single quality system. This simplifies document management, QC consistency, and logistics coordination for buyers launching a full product range.

Which certifications matter for private-label feminine care imports?

For reusables, request ISO 13485, FDA establishment registration or 510(k) evidence, CE marking, and ISO 10993 biocompatibility records. For disposables, request ISO 9001, FDA cosmetics or device registration, CE marking, SGS or TÜV test reports, and OEKO-TEX or GOTS for organic claims.

How do I evaluate a private-label feminine care supplier's quality system?

Request the supplier's ISO certificate scope, review sample QC records from recent production batches, ask about raw material inspection protocols, check whether in-process and final inspection checkpoints are documented, and request evidence of batch traceability from material receipt to shipment.

What information should I send for a private-label feminine care RFQ?

Send target market and channel, product type (cup, pad, disc, accessory), quantity forecast per SKU, preferred materials or ingredients, packaging direction (wrapper, box, pouch, language), color and logo requirements, sample quantity, launch timeline, and required certification or document list.
