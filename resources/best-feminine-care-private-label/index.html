Procurement Overview

China's private-label feminine care supplier landscape.

China's feminine care private-label market includes specialized silicone manufacturers (menstrual cups and discs), absorbent hygiene product factories (sanitary pads and liners), and full-range suppliers that produce both categories. Buyers launching a branded feminine care line should evaluate suppliers across product scope, material control, certification coverage, packaging integration, and quality system maturity.

Supplier archetype 2: Category-specialized manufacturer.

This archetype may suit a single-category program. Verify actual product expertise through the quoted specification, samples, process and QC records; do not assume specialization means stronger evidence, pricing, or compliance.

Best fit for: Buyers launching a single-category product line who want category-specific depth, specialized material expertise, and potentially lower pricing within that product type.

Procurement caution: Assess whether managing separate suppliers for each category aligns with your quality system, document management, and logistics resources. Multi-supplier programs require more coordination during sampling, production, and shipping.

Supplier archetype 3: Diversified hygiene product group.

This archetype may suit standardized, higher-volume programs. Verify the exact production line, quoted SKU, private-label minimum, changeover controls, evidence scope, and packaging flexibility rather than inferring fit from group size.

Best fit for: Retail chains and institutional buyers with high-volume forecasts and standardized product specifications who prioritize unit cost over format flexibility.

Procurement caution: Verify the manufacturer's minimum engagement level for private-label programs versus branded production, confirm format and material change flexibility, and review whether feminine care receives dedicated production line allocation.

How to select the right private-label supplier for your program.

  1. Map your product roadmap. Define current and planned SKUs, but verify each new category independently before assuming a multi-category supplier reduces risk or switching cost.
  2. Compare quality-system scope. If a certificate is relevant, verify holder, site, standard, covered activities or products, issue date, expiry date, and current status—not only a certificate number.
  3. Evaluate packaging handoff. Confirm who owns design, revisions, languages, claims, barcode, print approval, carton data, and final inspection; do not request confidential client files.
  4. Review document management. Assess whether the supplier can provide consistent specification sheets, QC records, and certificate packages across all your product types.
  5. Test communication and controls. Record RFQ clarity, open questions, document status, owners, and response dates; early responsiveness is useful evidence but not a production guarantee.

Buyer FAQ

Common questions about feminine care private label in China.

What feminine care products can suppliers in China private-label?

Chinese suppliers may quote menstrual cups and discs, sanitary pads and liners, intimate-care formats, reusable pouches, starter kits, and related accessories. Buyers should confirm the exact product, dimensions, materials, claims, manufacturing route, and evidence scope for every quoted SKU.

How should buyers confirm MOQ for a feminine care private-label program?

Ask for a written MOQ by product category and SKU, identifying product structure, materials, color, packaging, tooling, and customization assumptions. Do not assume that a multi-product program has a combined or universal MOQ.

Can one supplier handle my full feminine care product range?

A supplier may quote multiple categories, but buyers should verify each product's manufacturing route, specification, material, QC plan, evidence set, packaging responsibility, and market requirements separately. One company-level system or contact does not prove every SKU has the same controls or status.

Which certifications matter for private-label feminine care imports?

There is no universal certification list across cups, discs, pads, wipes, and accessories. Start with intended use, product classification, destination market, operator roles, and proposed claims. Then verify the exact quality-system, material, test, registration, or scheme evidence that applies; US registration is not FDA approval, and CE marking is used only when applicable EU legislation specifically requires it.

How do I evaluate a private-label feminine care supplier's quality system?

Request the supplier's ISO certificate scope, review sample QC records from recent production batches, ask about raw material inspection protocols, check whether in-process and final inspection checkpoints are documented, and request evidence of batch traceability from material receipt to shipment.

What information should I send for a private-label feminine care RFQ?

Send target market and channel, product type (cup, pad, disc, accessory), quantity forecast per SKU, preferred materials or ingredients, packaging direction (wrapper, box, pouch, language), color and logo requirements, sample quantity, launch timeline, and required certification or document list.
